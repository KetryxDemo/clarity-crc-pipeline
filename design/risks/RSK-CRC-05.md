---
itemId: RSK-CRC-05
itemType: Risk
itemTitle: False positive result leads to an unnecessary diagnostic colonoscopy
itemIsRiskControlledBy: SPEC-PIPE-REPORT, TC-AV-01
---

A specimen from a patient without colorectal cancer or advanced adenoma scores at or above
the positivity threshold and is reported POSITIVE. The patient undergoes diagnostic
colonoscopy, an invasive procedure carrying its own risks of perforation, bleeding, and
sedation-related harm, with no clinical indication.

## Item fields

### Cause

Threshold set to favour sensitivity at the cost of specificity; haemoglobin elevation from a
non-neoplastic source such as haemorrhoidal or menstrual bleeding; benign age-related
methylation contributing to the composite score.

### Risk control measures

The POSITIVE report states that colonoscopy is indicated as a diagnostic follow-up to a
screening result, framing it as investigation rather than as a finding of disease
(SPEC-PIPE-REPORT). Clinical specificity is established against a colonoscopy-confirmed
reference panel during analytical validation, and the threshold is set from that data with
the sensitivity and specificity trade-off recorded (TC-AV-01).
